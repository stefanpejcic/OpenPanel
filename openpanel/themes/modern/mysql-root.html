New password

8 to 30 characters. It has to pass the strength check your hosting provider set.

  • 8+ characters
  • 12+ characters
  • Lowercase
  • Uppercase
  • Number
  • Symbol

Who signs in as root

The panel and phpMyAdmin switch to the new password on their own. Anything else that connects as root needs it from you.

  • OpenPanelDatabase pages read it from my.cnf
  • phpMyAdminSigns you in as root with MYSQL_ROOT_PASSWORD from .env
  • Remote AccessOff on this account. When on, root can also sign in from outside on port 32770
    Uses the new password
  • Your apps and scriptsAnything you set up yourself to connect as root
    Update by hand

Changing root password

  1. Set the password for root@'%' and root@'localhost'ALTER USER, twice
  2. Reload privilegesFLUSH PRIVILEGES
  3. Save it for the panel and phpMyAdminmy.cnf and .env

Change the root password?

MariaDB 13.0.2 on this account

  • The panel and phpMyAdmin switch to the new password right away.
  • MariaDB keeps running. Nothing restarts and your sites' own database users aren't touched.
  • Apps, scripts or remote clients that sign in as root with the old password stop connecting until you update them.
