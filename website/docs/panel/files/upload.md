# Upload from Device

The **Upload from Device** page allows you to easily upload files from your local device to a selected directory on the server.

![File Upload page with the drag and drop area and the Upload button](/img/openpanel-screenshots/files/upload-form.png#gh-light-mode-only)
![File Upload page with the drag and drop area and the Upload button](/img/openpanel-screenshots/files/upload-form_dark.png#gh-dark-mode-only)

The quickest way to upload is from the [File Manager](/docs/panel/files/files/) itself: click **Upload** in its toolbar, or drag files onto the file list, and they are uploaded into the folder you are viewing.

This page is still available at `/file-manager/upload`. To use it:

1. Navigate to the desired directory.
2. Drag and drop files or select them manually from your device.
3. Click **Upload** to begin.

A progress bar will display the status of each file. You can upload multiple files at once.

> ⚠️ The upload size limit is configured by the administrator.

For large files (over 1GB), we recommend using **FTP** or the **Download from URL** feature instead.
