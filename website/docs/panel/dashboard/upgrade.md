# Upgrade

When your hosting provider offers an upgrade plan for your account, the dashboard shows two tabs, **Dashboard** and **Upgrade now**, and an **Upgrade** link appears at the bottom of the sidebar.

The **Upgrade now** tab compares your current plan with the upgrade plan:

- **Left**: the features the upgrade plan adds, grouped by the menu area they appear in.
- **Right**: your current plan's limits next to the upgrade plan's limits. A higher limit is marked with **↑**, a lower one with **↓**, and **∞** means unlimited. Limits are only listed for features included in at least one of the two plans.

Click **Upgrade to &lt;plan&gt;** to open your hosting provider's upgrade page in a new tab.

Features that are not included in your plan, but are included in the upgrade plan, are shown greyed out with a lock icon in the menu and on the dashboard. Clicking one of them opens this page.

:::info
Upgrade plans are set by the administrator in the hosting plan settings and are only available on the Enterprise edition. Without an upgrade plan, the dashboard has no tabs and the Upgrade link is not shown.
:::
