# Webmail

Click the **Webmail** link next to an email address to autologin to Roundcube webmail. It opens in a new browser tab.

![Webmail, Manage and Connect Devices buttons in the row of an email account](/img/openpanel-screenshots/emails/emails-buttons.png)

:::info
If the webmail service is not running, you'll see a notice asking you to contact your administrator, and you'll be redirected back to the Emails page.
:::
