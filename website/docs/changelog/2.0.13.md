### 💅 Polish
- `opencli domains-test` and `opencli domains-cloudflare` commands.
- [Applications & Dashboard Widget UX Suggestions #1157](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/OpenPanel/discussions/1157)
- OpenAdmin: all pages with tables can now be sorted by clicking the arrows next to the column names.
- CorazaWAF no longer inspects response bodies (`SecResponseBodyAccess Off` in `/etc/openpanel/caddy/coraza_rules.conf`).
- `opencli waf enable` now disables the OWASP CRS rulesets for Java, Ruby and IIS (`REQUEST-944`, `RESPONSE-952`, `RESPONSE-954`, `RESPONSE-956`).
- `opencli sentinel --startup` now starts user containers in stages after a reboot (a few users at a time, waiting on high IO/load) instead of all at once, avoiding an IO storm on servers with many users.
- `opencli user-backup` now runs tar and compression at low CPU/IO priority (`nice -n19`, `ionice -c2 -n7`) so live sites stay responsive during backups, `--no-throttle` runs it at full speed.
