Not yet released.

### 💡 New
- `opencli waf enable` now downloads the official OWASP CRS rule exclusion plugins (WordPress, Drupal, Nextcloud, DokuWiki, phpBB, XenForo, phpMyAdmin), and `opencli waf update` keeps them updated.
- `opencli waf plugins` lists installed CRS plugins and how many domains use each, `opencli waf plugins install` downloads missing ones.
- `opencli waf domain <DOMAIN> plugins` shows the protection level and the app profiles turned on for a domain.
- OpenPanel > WAF: redesigned domain page with *App profiles* that can be turned on per domain, recommended automatically based on the apps installed on the domain. [WAF UX Suggestion #1156](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/OpenPanel/discussions/1156)
- OpenPanel > WAF: per-domain protection level (Compatibility, Standard, Strict).
- OpenPanel > WAF: *Monitor only* mode that logs attacks without blocking them, also `opencli waf domain <DOMAIN> monitor`.
- OpenPanel > WAF: new *Recent blocked requests* section on the domain page that explains blocked requests in plain words, grouped by rule, with *Disable rule* and *Undo*, and a link to the full logs.
- OpenPanel > WAF: *Set Level* and *Apply Profile* bulk actions, and filters for status, level and profile.
- Installing WordPress, Drupal, Nextcloud, phpBB or DokuWiki (or importing them with *Scan*) now turns on the matching WAF profile when the user has the WAF module.
- Changing WAF profiles, the protection level or disabling a rule on the domain's WAF page can now be undone from the confirmation toast.
- OpenPanel > WAF: switch to turn the firewall on or off for all domains at once, also saved as the default for new domains, and new table columns for blocked requests (24h), protection level and app profiles.

### 💅 Polish
- `opencli domains-test` and `opencli domains-cloudflare` commands.
- [Applications & Dashboard Widget UX Suggestions #1157](https://github.com/stefanpejcic/OpenPanel/discussions/1157)
- OpenAdmin: all pages with tables can now be sorted by clicking the arrows next to the column names.
- CorazaWAF no longer inspects response bodies (`SecResponseBodyAccess Off` in `/etc/openpanel/caddy/coraza_rules.conf`).
- `opencli waf enable` now disables the OWASP CRS rulesets for Java, Ruby and IIS (`REQUEST-944`, `RESPONSE-952`, `RESPONSE-954`, `RESPONSE-956`).
- `opencli sentinel --startup` now starts user containers in stages after a reboot (a few users at a time, waiting on high IO/load) instead of all at once, avoiding an IO storm on servers with many users.
- `opencli user-backup` now runs tar and compression at low CPU/IO priority (`nice -n19`, `ionice -c2 -n7`) so live sites stay responsive during backups, `--no-throttle` runs it at full speed.
