### 🐛 Bug Fixes
- Varnish: error responses (5xx) are no longer cached. After a reboot nginx can come up before PHP-FPM, and the 502 it returned in those few seconds was cached for an hour (plus 6 hours of grace) and served to every visitor even after PHP was running. Varnish now never caches a 5xx, and when a background refresh fails it keeps serving the last good copy. Applies to new Varnish installs, an existing site picks it up after its `default.vcl` is updated.
